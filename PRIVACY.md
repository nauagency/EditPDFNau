# EditPDFNau Privacy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

EditPDFNau opens, edits, and saves PDF documents locally. The standalone PDF
application does not send product analytics to GenOffice or Genspark and does
not require an account with either service.

AI features are optional. When you invoke them, requests are sent only to the
provider you configured, such as Codex CLI, a local OpenAI-compatible endpoint,
or a provider for which you supplied credentials. Review that provider's privacy
policy before use. Do not submit sensitive documents to a remote provider unless
you are authorized to do so.

Credentials and EditPDFNau settings are stored in the application's local user
data directory. PDF files are not uploaded merely by opening or editing them.
